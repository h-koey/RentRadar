# Part 1: Product Scope and User Experience

### 1.1. Final Product Scope

**Startup Name:** Illini Rent Radar: Are You Paying Too Much?

Illini Rent Radar is an application that helps University of Illinois students and community members make informed choices about off-campus housing decisions. Users are able to search historical apartment lease information, see fees that are easily missed in advertised prices, and view estimated/projected future rent and utility costs to help plan budgets before signing a lease.

Our original startup idea + wireframes from P2-A2 remain mostly the same. We are still focused on helping renters understand the overall cost of living in an apartment instead of looking at advertised monthly rent alone.

**Problem Being Solved:**

Having to rent off campus in Urbana-Champaign means having to compare many buildings and leasing companies as early as a year before moving in, just to get the best options available on the market. Advertised rent is only part of the cost. Application, parking, service fees, amenity fees, and utility bills add up, and this makes it difficult to compare across apartments. Unfortunately, listing sites tend to emphasize advertised prices, so students often learn the full cost only after committing.

Illini Rent Radar aims to make this process easier by providing historical rental information and tools that help users estimate their total housing expenses.

**Target Users:**

Our main target users are University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign students who are searching for off-campus housing. The application can also be useful for other Urbana-Champaign residents who want to compare apartment prices, and explore rental information to get a better idea of their housing costs.

**Core Value Proposition:**

Illini Rent Radar helps renters make informed housing decisions by giving them a clearer picture of what an apartment might actually cost. Instead of comparing places solely based on monthly rent, users also get to consider additional expenses and look at historical rental trends.

### 1.2. Core Product Features

The application will focus on three main features:

1. **Apartment Lease Search:** Users can search & filter historical apartment rental information based on factors such as location, price, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and lease year.

2. **Total Housing Cost Calculator:** Users can combine monthly rent with estimated utility costs and other additional fees to get an idea of their total monthly and yearly housing expenses.

3. **Rent Trends and Predictions:** Users can explore historical rental price trends and view estimated future rent costs based on historical rental information and AI-powered analysis.

### 1.3. User Experience

Illini Rent Radar is designed for the Urbana-Champaign community to have a simple and accessible experience whilst searching for off-campus housing.

The application will have a clear & same navigation bar and a consistent layout so users can easily find and use the site's different features. Users will be able to do the following: search for apartment information, enter their own details, calculate estimated housing expenses (with flexibility on adding extra expenses), and view relevant results to their needs.

Overall, we want to make comparing apartments and understanding housing costs as straightforward and convenient as possible.

### 1.4. Product Differentiation

Illini Rent Radar is different because it focuses on the Champaign-Urbana off-campus housing market. The application brings together historical rental information, estimated additional housing costs, and rent trend predictions in one place.

Rather than having renters focus only on advertised apartment prices, we want to help them get a better idea of the total cost they may be paying. This gives students and other renters more information to compare their options, plan their budgets, and make housing decisions with greater confidence.
