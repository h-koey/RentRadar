# Part 1: Product Scope and User Experience

### 1.1. Final Product Scope

**Startup Name:** Illini Rent Radar: Are You Paying Too Much?

Illini Rent Radar is an application designed to help University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign students and other Champaign-Urbana residents make better-informed decisions when looking for off-campus housing. The application allows users to explore historical apartment rental information, estimate additional housing expenses, and get a better understanding of rental price trends.

Our original startup idea and wireframes from P2-A2 remain mostly the same. We are still focused on helping renters understand the overall cost of living in an apartment instead of looking at advertised monthly rent alone.

**Problem Being Solved:**

Finding affordable off-campus housing can be difficult because the advertised rent does not always show how much someone will actually spend each month. Extra expenses such as utilities, parking, application fees, and amenity fees can add up quickly, making it harder for renters to compare apartments and stay within their budgets.

Illini Rent Radar aims to make this process easier by providing historical rental information and tools that help users estimate their total housing expenses.

**Target Users:**

Our main target users are University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign students who are searching for off-campus housing. However, the application can also be useful for other Champaign-Urbana residents who want to compare apartment prices, explore rental information, and get a better idea of their housing costs.

**Core Value Proposition:**

Illini Rent Radar helps renters make more informed housing decisions by giving them a clearer picture of what an apartment might actually cost. Instead of comparing places based only on monthly rent, users can also consider additional expenses and look at historical rental trends, all in one place.

### 1.2. Core Product Features

The application will focus on three main features:

1. **Apartment Lease Search:** Users can search and filter historical apartment rental information based on factors such as location, price, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and lease year.

2. **Total Housing Cost Calculator:** Users can combine monthly rent with estimated utility costs and other additional fees to get an idea of their total monthly and yearly housing expenses.

3. **Rent Trends and Predictions:** Users can explore historical rental price trends and view estimated future rent costs based on historical rental information and AI-powered analysis.

### 1.3. User Experience

Illini Rent Radar is designed to provide a simple and accessible experience for people searching for off-campus housing.

The application will have a clear navigation bar and a consistent layout so users can easily find and use its different features. Users will be able to search for apartment information, enter their own details, calculate estimated housing expenses, and view results that are relevant to their needs.

Overall, we want to make comparing apartments and understanding housing costs as straightforward and convenient as possible.

### 1.4. Product Differentiation

What makes Illini Rent Radar different is its focus on the Champaign-Urbana off-campus housing market. The application brings together historical rental information, estimated additional housing costs, and rent trend predictions in one place.

Rather than having renters focus only on advertised apartment prices, we want to help them get a better idea of the total cost they may be paying. This gives students and other renters more information to compare their options, plan their budgets, and make housing decisions with greater confidence.
