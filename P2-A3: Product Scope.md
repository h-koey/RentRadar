# Part 1: Product Scope and User Experience

### 1.1. Final Product Scope

**Startup Name:** Illini Rent Radar: Are You Paying Too Much?

Illini Rent Radar is a web application designed to help University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign students and Champaign-Urbana community members make more informed off-campus housing decisions. The application allows users to explore historical apartment rental information, estimate additional housing expenses, and understand rental price trends.

Our original startup idea and wireframes from P2-A2 remain largely unchanged. The project continues to focus on helping renters better understand the total cost of housing rather than relying only on advertised monthly rent.

**Problem Being Solved:**

Finding affordable off-campus housing can be challenging because advertised rent does not always reflect the full cost of living in an apartment. Additional expenses such as utilities, parking, application fees, and amenity fees can make it difficult for renters to compare housing options and plan their budgets.

Illini Rent Radar aims to address this problem by providing historical rental information and tools to estimate total housing expenses.

**Target Users:**

Our primary target users are University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign students searching for off-campus housing. The application may also benefit other Champaign-Urbana residents who want to compare apartment prices and better understand their housing expenses.

**Core Value Proposition:**

Illini Rent Radar helps renters make more informed housing decisions by providing a clearer picture of apartment costs. Instead of comparing apartments based only on advertised rent, users can consider additional expenses and historical rental trends in one place.

### 1.2. Core Product Features

The application will include three primary features:

1. **Apartment Lease Search:** Users can search and filter historical apartment rental information by location, price, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and lease year.

2. **Total Housing Cost Calculator:** Users can combine monthly rent with estimated utilities and additional fees to calculate their estimated monthly and yearly housing expenses.

3. **Rent Trends and Predictions:** Users can explore historical rental price trends and view estimated future rent costs using historical rental information and AI or statistical techniques.

### 1.3. User Experience

Illini Rent Radar aims to provide a straightforward and accessible experience for users searching for off-campus housing.

The application will include clear navigation, a consistent interface, and functional forms that allow users to search for apartment information and calculate estimated housing expenses. Users will be able to submit information, interact with the application's features, and view relevant results.

The overall goal is to make comparing apartment costs and understanding housing expenses simple and convenient.

### 1.4. Product Differentiation

Illini Rent Radar focuses on the Champaign-Urbana off-campus housing market and combines historical rental information, additional housing cost estimates, and rent trend predictions.

Rather than focusing only on advertised apartment prices, the application aims to help students understand the potential total cost of housing. By bringing these features together, Illini Rent Radar provides a convenient way for renters to compare housing options and make more informed decisions.
